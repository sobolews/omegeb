# [Hundo 1.9v2 Route](https://celer.pistonite.org/view/sobolews/omegeb:1.9v2) Changelog

These are all the changes since the [previous route I ran and documented](https://celer.pistonite.org/view/sobolews/omegeb:v1_new_celer).
Some of these changes are already years old, but were never properly documented, some are related to version 1.9 patching BLSS, and some are because the run has gotten fast enough that several time-of-day and fury timing cycles were breaking.

This route is intended for latest patch, and since the fastest version of that is 1.9 Switch 1 Edition run on a Switch 2, I've removed references to superlaunches and unloaded shrines/caves as they can't happen on S2 hardware.

**Differences from the previous 1.6 route**:


- Plateau: BLSS no longer available, so revert to SCMtB plat. (working on timing some other options though...)

- BLSS to Satori Mountain replaced with BTB to the same place (most other long BLSSes are replaced with midair chains)

- Moved R60 to Naboris branch

- Moved R53 and C36 to get on the way from Satori to Castle.

- Added a headshot lizalfos BTB to get to Castle. (note: you can make this BTB easier with ice arrows, but then you'll need a different, slightly slower, [IST setup](https://tinyurl.com/y8acrbp8))

- Moved Goponga Village Ruins to Tabantha Tower split

- IST is nearly the same, but you can get 6 Lotuses instead of 7 and skip cycling them in one spot, and now cooking speedfood is always done in Hateno, so it's not affected by rain.

- Ask for 9 stamina and 7 hearts at the Kakariko Goddess Statue, and request a stamina at the Horned Statue (saves a couple seconds of text)

- House upgrade is now done earlier (or you can wait till hateno2 if you're fast enough to worry about time of day being too early for the Hebra Salavager Chest)

- Cooking the WMC meal is now done in Hateno instead of Flight Range.

- WMC itself is the same, but the prep looks slightly different because we're dropping the korok seeds at the Rito cookpot instead of Flight Range.

- Rito General Shop is moved after WMC instead of before Medoh. (less menuing in WMC)

- Moved T17 + Rito Hinox from Rito2 to Rito1, and moved T10 + T13 from Rito1 to Rito2

- Moved P09 (SoR korok) to Naboris branch. (makes it easier to kill Plat talus before bloodmoon happens)

- Moved A37 and two wetlands Stalkers from Dah Hesho to new Shae Mosah (time of day for Dugby)

- Moved buying Goron Spice from first Goron City visit to third (time of day for Dugby)
  - This means you can discover Stolock Bridge by midairing from E15 instead of dropping down on the way to E14

- Moved completion of Shae Mosah to the second Goron City visit, but still activate it in first visit. (time of day for Dugby)

- Moved A34, A38 from a later Tarrey Town visit to the new Shae Mosah split (time of day for Dugby)

- Swapped R03 / H71 (removes gale)

- Moved R15 from Toh Yahsa to Zalta Wa (better movement)

- Moved C37 from Kah Okeo to Sheem Dagoze

- Moved Jeddo Bridge a bit later, use Gale to go R48 -> R39

- Moved R56, R55, R54, R62, R61, R52, Satori Stalnox from Kah Okeo to Mijah Rokee (more bloodmoon buffer, and fury timing for plateau talus, and reduces chances of Lord of the Mountain requiring a reload)

- Moved P18 from plateau1 to plateau2, and warp to bombs insteaad of stasis (because it's not activated in SCMtB plateau route)

- Re-ordered plateau talus and P04 in Kaam Yatak split (fury timing for plat talus)

- Moved C61, C60 from Rota Ooh to Central Tower (Thunder Magnet quest timing)

- G26 - can use spare fury to clear the annoying enemies

- Moved W19, Kay Noh, W15 from gerudo1 to gerudo2

- Moved D13, Horwell Bridge, D25, D29, D28, D24, Nabi Talus, D15, D23 from Hila Rao to Bosh Kala (Thunder Magnet quest timing)

- Outpost Talus - changed fury strategy

- Moved Big Twin, Little Twin, Misko, and Wild Horses quests from endgame to Ha Dahamar (Thunder Magnet quest timing)

- Moved F46, F17 from Faron1 to Faron2 (fury timing for Palmorae talus)

- Moved F34 from Shai Utoh to Shoda Sah (fury timing for Palmorae talus)

- Moved F21, F29, Qukah Nata, Kass from after Tawa Jinn to before (better movement)

- Moved Eagus Bridge, D01 from after Hila Rao to before

- Removed gales at Namika Ozz and T12

- Reordered most of Rito2 NPCs/koroks/shrine

- Reordered H10, H09, H11, H04 (better movement)

- Moved H20 from Goma Asaagh to small Hebra North branch (better movement)

- Moved A44, A35, A36, A33, Ritaag Zumo to the previous branch (time of day for Tumbo)

- Moved K09 from before Rona Kachta to after, using gale instead of BLSS

- Added dynamic map% calcuation to the route (all map%'s are recalculated by the celer engine any time the route changes, so no more having to complete a run to learn the correct map% checkpoints - thanks Savage!)

- and many small clarifications or rewordings
